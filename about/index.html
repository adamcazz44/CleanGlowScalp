About

No plugs, no drugs,
no rugs.

That's the whole philosophy. Here's how I got to it.

I started losing my hair at 20. For a decade I did the awkward dance — the self-conscious shuffle of a guy who's "too young to be losing it." At 30 I stopped fighting and shaved it all off, and I never looked back.

What surprised me was the upgrade. The hesitation got replaced with a level of machismo I didn't expect. The clinging-on years felt worse than anything that came after.

And honestly, the look's everywhere now. You can spot the guys who haven't surrendered yet from a mile away — the combover, the growth products on the bathroom shelf, the ball cap that never comes off. Trust me: hanging on looks so much worse than just getting rid of it and getting on with your life.

Then nobody tells you the rest

Here's what they don't mention: once the hair's gone, the scalp becomes the thing you actually have to look after. And you find out the hard way. The shine in every photo. The patch you missed because you can't see the back of your own head. Nicks. Trying to get it even with no eyes on the back. Standing in the shower wondering whether you should be using real shaving cream, a gel, or just the conditioner you've got in your hand. Sun spots, eventually, where the sun hits hardest.

Nobody handed me a manual for any of that. So this site is the manual — the stuff I had to figure out, sorted out for you.

The whole point

Get rid of it and get on with your life. We've got your scalp covered, and by God you'll have the best-kept head in the building. Hold it high. Then put your energy into the parts you actually control — get fit, build some muscle, whiten the teeth, grow a solid beard, dress sharper. The hair was never the thing.

Straight with you

I'm a guy who shaves his head — not a dermatologist. So when something on this site touches your health, I cite real medical sources, and I tell you plainly when a problem is a job for a doctor instead of a product. See the not-medical-advice note for exactly where I draw that line. No invented experts, no fake reviews, no recommendations I wouldn't use myself.

— Adam
