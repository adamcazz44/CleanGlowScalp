Privacy

This page explains what Clean Glow Scalp collects and why. Plain version: we don't ask you for personal information, and we don't sell anything about you.

What's collected

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are read and how people find the site. This is aggregate traffic data — it isn't used to identify you personally.

If we later add ads (for example, Google AdSense), advertising partners may use cookies to show relevant ads. When that happens, this page will be updated to say so and you'll be able to manage your choices.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the destination retailer may set a cookie so any purchase is credited to us. That's handled by the retailer, under their privacy policy, not ours.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the contact details published on the site.

This is a starting policy and should be reviewed before launch to match the exact tools and contact details in use.
